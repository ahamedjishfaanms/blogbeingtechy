About·BeingTechy

Signal from the tech world, no noise.

BeingTechy is an independent publication covering software, hardware, and the people building both — written for people who build things, not just read about them.

What we do

We publish articles on artificial intelligence, hardware, developer tools, and security — the topics that shape how technology actually gets built. Our focus is on clear, practical writing rather than hype: explainers that hold up, field notes from real tooling, and analysis that tries to be useful a year from now, not just this week.

Who we are

BeingTechy is run as an independent, self-published outlet. We're not affiliated with any single hardware vendor, cloud provider, or software company, and we don't accept payment in exchange for favorable coverage. Where an article discusses a specific product or company, that's a reflection of what's relevant to our readers, not a sponsorship.

How we make money

BeingTechy is supported by display advertising, including ads served through Google AdSense. Advertising helps us keep the site free to read and independently written. You can find more detail on the data this involves in our Privacy Policy.

Get in touch

Have a correction, a tip, or a question about something we published? We'd like to hear from you — visit our Contact page for the best way to reach us.
